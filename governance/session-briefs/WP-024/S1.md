- Actual approval/dependency/independence missing; governing contracts conflict; source identity changes unexpectedly; mandatory checks are empty, unavailable or deferred.

- Execution without accepted containment, undeclared network/provisioning, hidden holdout access, or a request to retry until a favorable outcome.
- Cheap verifier selected outside its semantic support
- Budget exhaustion or retries biasing verdicts toward apparent success

## Boundaries by decision

- **Routine implementation:** a disclosed delegate may choose details inside
  actually authorized scope, preserving requirements, tests, safety and science.
- **Ordinary scope/readiness/administrative closure:** AMD-002 permits the
  principal or a disclosed authorized delegate only after every actual required
  dependency/evidence/review condition is met. The delegated approver must be
  genuinely separate from the implementation author.
- **Audit and protocol review:** a genuinely independent context must read and
  assess actual source/evidence. Delegation cannot substitute an author's
  self-review, erase a verdict or supply missing EXP-E2 retrospectively.
- **Findings:** D1/D2 cannot be carried. Required independent verification,
  valid amendment/decline and bounded lower-severity dispositions remain.
- **Constitution, material expansion and material risks:** retain Michael's
  reserved decision authority; routine delegation cannot expand itself.
- **EV-E6 and release:** retain their separately required review and approval
  conditions; a routine delegation is not correspondence/release acceptance.
- **Consequential external actions:** pushes/publication, payments, installations,
  remote source disclosure and signing credentials need separate exact-scope
  authorization. No hosted CI; local-only checks remain mandatory.

## Disclosed approval identity

A delegated decision must identify the real delegate/context, principal's
delegation basis, scope, exact source/declaration/evidence digests, rationale,
time, restrictions and independence checks. It must not say Michael personally
reviewed evidence unless he actually did. No unknown future commit hash is
invented to satisfy a circular record field.

Constitutional ratification is now real; package acceptance is not thereby real.
WP-001 remains the only active package and its S1 remains open. Subsequent
approval, audit, finding and experiment records must establish their own gates.

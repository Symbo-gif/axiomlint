## Assessment

  evidence-carrying review of small typed Python fragments. It separates model
  theorems, executable failures and source correspondence; no-finding is not proof.
- **Method:** S1-S4 order, actual independent S2, finding-only S3, separate
  commits, explicit approvals, retention and preregistered experiment controls
  remain necessary. Author tests do not constitute the missing audit.
- **Local-only tailoring:** AMD-001 records the upstream authoritative-CI
  wording conflict rather than concealing it. Exact-revision local acceptance,
  independent replay and approved closure compensate operationally, but do
  not establish unqualified upstream compliance or hosted-build guarantees.
- **Delegation conflict:** the current charter reserves formal approvals and
  disallows self-approval. DEC-004 records the real grant within that boundary;
  AMD-002 proposes a prospective, disclosed delegation rule. This author does
  not mark their own proposed authority amendment ratified.
- **Scientific rigor:** independent EXP-E2 must precede measurement, held-out
  answers must stay isolated, and outcome-dependent method changes cannot be
  relabeled confirmatory. The calibration/corpus ordering decision remains real.

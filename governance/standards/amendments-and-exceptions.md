# Amendment, exception and baseline procedure

Status: WP-001 S1 procedural implementation of the existing ratified charter,
AMD-001/AMD-002 and methodology. It adds no power to waive their hard controls.

## Declare and bind the change

Every proposed change identifies its ID, owner, baseline commit/document digest,
conflicting clause, proposed replacement, rationale, affected requirements and
packages, scope/non-goals, risks, tests/evidence, documentation, rollback and
effective/sunset conditions. Use the existing amendment and approval templates.

Freeze the reviewed text and obtain the actual required decision before using
the replacement. Record principal or real delegate/context, authority basis,
independence, exact source/artifact digests, date, restrictions and evidence.
Do not fill an approval because a test passed or because an assistant wrote it.
The implementer cannot approve their own material scope extension or readiness.

## Decision routes

| Situation | Required treatment |
|---|---|
| Routine detail inside approved scope | Disclosed technical judgment; preserve requirements and verification |
| Ordinary delegated scope/readiness/administrative closure | All AMD-002 conditions, genuinely separate approver context and source-bound evidence |
| Constitutional/delegation expansion or material scope/risk | Michael's reserved actual decision; no self-expansion |
| D3/D4 bounded carry where permitted | Actual independent/authorized disposition, owner, reason, expiry, compensating controls and downstream limits |
| D1/D2 | No carry; affected package/claim/release stays blocked pending valid resolution |
| Missing execution/isolation capability | Refuse/disable the affected execution; no best-effort assurance |
| Unknown material license | Quarantine/not adopt; no mandatory-core exception |
| Post-result protocol/threshold/seed/denominator change | New protocol/experiment or explicitly exploratory analysis; never rewrite frozen confirmatory history |
| Accepted-evidence correction | New linked correction/erratum with retained prior bytes, not in-place replacement |
| Public/sensitive external action | Separate exact-scope authorization; an internal policy decision does not authorize execution |

## Findings are not risk preferences

An open finding needs an actual disposition with evidence. A demonstrated false
positive may be independently declined with reasoning; that is not a shortcut
for carrying a real D1/D2. A policy amendment may change a future requirement
only through its proper authority; it cannot retroactively make failed evidence
pass. Material residual-risk acceptance remains reserved.

A normative requirement, a planning instruction and an engineering observation
are different. If a lower brief's generic stop language conflicts with the
normative methodology's D2 package-closure rule, record the conflict and obtain
an independent authorized interpretation. Do not let the author silently
resolve their own entry/readiness gate.

## Distinct sessions and exact-revision evidence

S1-S4 stay ordered, separate and recorded, even if S3 has no product changes.
An independent read-only review is not merged into implementation. S3 fixes
only actual findings; S4 cannot hide product changes as documentation.

Commit a candidate before clean local acceptance. An approval binds that
actual commit and manifest, not an unknown future hash. If later records change
the head, verify those records/head separately; never inherit old logs without
declared scope. Store external attestations where needed to avoid self-reference.
Unavailable metadata/schema support is a finding/blocker, not permission to
invent identities.

## Retention, revocation and rollback

Preserve prior declarations, approvals, failed/negative/null/inconclusive
observations, rejected requests, findings, waivers and revocations. Link
superseding records to their predecessors. Roll back capability/source or
quarantine artifacts without erasing accepted history.

Expire an exception when its stated time, revision, scope or assumptions cease
to hold. Reassess affected downstream claims and stop uncovered work; an old
waiver is not permanent authority. Record who revoked/replaced it and why.

Ratification APR-001 is complete and is not requested again. DEV-001/DEV-002
remain open until their actual independent dispositions; this procedure neither
verifies them nor treats them as permitted D2 carry.
